CONNECTING
CCS, DIGITALLY.

Trimex Connect is a redesigned digital gateway created to make important CCS resources, services, announcements, and opportunities easier to find and access.

TC 2026
THE CONCEPT

Less searching.
More connecting.

The redesigned Trimex Connect interface focuses on creating a cleaner and more accessible digital experience for the CCS community.

Instead of presenting services as a simple list, the design organizes them into clear sections so students can quickly find academic tools, student services, opportunities, and important CCS information.
